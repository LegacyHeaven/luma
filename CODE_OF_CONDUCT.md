# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want LUMA's community, issues, and discussions to be welcoming and
harassment-free for everyone, regardless of experience, background, or
identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate in comments, issues, and pull requests.
- Give constructive feedback; focus on the idea, not the person.
- Accept disagreement gracefully.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and
other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct, and may ban
contributors for behavior they deem inappropriate.

Report a violation by opening a
[GitHub issue](../../issues/new/choose) or contacting a maintainer directly
through GitHub.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
